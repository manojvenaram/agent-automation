# SensorySlice (vido) — Autonomous YouTube Shorts Agent

## Vision
An autonomous, zero-cost AI media company and content generation platform producing viral vertical short-form videos with zero human input.

## Goals
1. Autonomous trend discovery across Reddit, Google Trends, HackerNews, and Wikipedia.
2. Scientific fact-checking and automated script revision.
3. Multi-engine video rendering (Cinematic, Brainrot Split-Screen, Stickman, Manim).
4. Edge-TTS neural voice synthesis with word-accurate Whisper subtitle sync.
5. Persistent self-learning via Content Brain and Daily Review.

## Non-Goals
- Manual timeline editing.
- Expensive paid subscription APIs.
- Generic low-quality image slideshows.
