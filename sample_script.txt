Why does space smell like burnt popcorn? 🌌🍿 Whenever astronauts return from a spacewalk and take off their helmets, they notice a distinct smoky, metallic aroma. NASA scientists found this scent comes from polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons—high-energy molecules produced by dying stars floating across the void.
